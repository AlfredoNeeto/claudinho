4. The `statusLine` command shape differs from the plan's door 4 literal (`C:/Users/<user>/AppData/Local/Claudinho/claudinho.exe statusline`) - `pc/Claudinho/Installer.cs:70-77` writes `~/AppData/Local/Claudinho/claudinho.exe statusline`, and `pc/Claudinho.Tests/InstallTests.cs:55` asserts it. But a one-way-door literal changed without being recorded under "Settled mid-build", and C24's `<exe com />` is too vague to catch it. Whether PowerShell expands `~` was not checked.
5. C6 does not test the mutex - `pc/Claudinho.Tests/StatuslineTests.cs:103-107` injects `running`. `BridgeHost.Running` (`pc/Claudinho/Bridge.cs:91-96`) checks that the mutex *exists* (`TryOpenExisting`), not that it is *held*, and no test exercises it. This is a level gap.

7. C23's script accepts any `claudinho 1 *` reply (`pc/tools/handshake.ps1:23`), weaker than the check's `cyd`. The observed output was `claudinho 1 cyd`.
8. C28 hides only `DOTNET_ROOT` and `PATH` (`pc/tools/check-publish.ps1:15-16`). A framework-dependent apphost still finds a runtime through the registry or `C:\Program Files\dotnet`, so the "no .NET" step proves little on its own. The 94.5 MB size and `--self-contained` at `:5` carry the claim, and neither is asserted.
9. C15's scanner only matches 6-digit hex literals (`pc/tools/check_palette.py:14`). RGB565 or 8-digit literals and LVGL helpers such as `lv_color_white()` or `lv_palette_*` would escape it. The only such case today is `firmware/src/main.cpp:45` `fillScreen(0x0000)` (black, harmless).
10. Some boundaries are not sampled: C34 at exactly 80% (only 86 is tested), C33 at used == pace, and C31's "janela que acaba primeiro" with two projecting windows. Low.
